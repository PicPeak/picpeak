# RAW fixture

`synthetic.dng` is the camera RAW the photo editor's RAW tests (issue 2619)
develop with LibRaw. It is not a photograph: `makeSyntheticDng.js` writes it
from nothing, a 384 x 256 RGGB Bayer mosaic of a known scene (a grey ramp,
six colour patches, a highlight that clips red first) with an as-shot white
balance of 2 / 1 / 1.43, sRGB primaries as the camera space, a capture date
(2026:08:01 14:30:00) and the camera name "PicPeak Synthetic RAW".

Source: generated by `node makeSyntheticDng.js` in this folder; the output
is deterministic. Licence: original work of the PicPeak project, MIT like
the rest of the repository. No third-party image data.
